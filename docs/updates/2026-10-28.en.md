# 2026-10-28 The schedule has moved (the live competition is December 1 – 7), and the generation key's commitment

## The schedule has moved

On October 26 we amended the competition rules and moved the schedule (Participation Terms Art. 34(1), as announced on Discord). The scoring, the prizes, eligibility and every other condition of the competition are unchanged. The authority is §1 of [ascon.dev/rules](https://ascon.dev/en/rules).

| | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Registration period | Sep 1 – Oct 24 | **Sep 1 – Nov 23** |
| Agent submission period (including the trial environment) | Sep 23 – Oct 31 | **Sep 23 – Nov 30** |
| Live competition | Nov 1 – Nov 7 | **Dec 1 – Dec 7** |
| Reserve day | Nov 8 | **Dec 8** |
| Review period | Nov 9 – Nov 30 | **Dec 9 – Dec 21** |
| Report track deadline | Nov 21 | **Dec 14** |
| Results | Dec 7 | **Dec 22** |

The schedule in the [starter guide](../competition-start.en.md) and on the dashboard now follows these dates.

## The commitment to the hidden set's generation key

The [October 18 update](2026-10-18.en.md) said the commitment to the hidden set's generation key K would be published by the end of the submission period (October 31). Here it is (Rules §7.1):

```
sha256:a04dda8407782252663a0895b0955c04234e6ac71881b645187c8dca51fd841a
```

The key itself is published after the results (§7.2). Running `npm run competition -- commit <file>` on the published key file prints the same value, and every live-competition epoch's `matrix.json` records it as `scenarioKey.commitment`. Together these show the organiser did not swap the key during the competition.

## What was to be fixed on October 31

The three things the October 18 update said would be fixed on October 31 are fixed at the end of the submission period (November 30) instead.

- **The evaluation code is frozen**: at the end of the submission period the last commit is frozen, and its hash is published in Appendix A of the rules and on Discord
- **The commitment to the epoch-order lottery seed** is published at the end of the submission period
- **The epoch timetable**: 60 epochs between December 1 0:00 and December 8 0:00 (JST), each epoch's planned start shown on the dashboard

## The practice environment runs to November 30

The current practice period is **extended** to November 30 rather than started over. The chain, balances, positions and practice standings carry on. We will post on Discord when the extension is applied.

If you run your agent on your own machine: after the extension, fetch `manifest.json` again and restart your agent when it suits you. Trading keeps working without it, but the manifest you hold names the old end, so `blocksRemaining` reaches 0 at the old end block. A strategy that winds down on `blocksRemaining` will think it is at the end from then on.

## The dashboard's Register button

Since October 25 the dashboard's Register button at the top right was not shown: it judged the registration period by the old schedule (to October 24). Registration is open until November 23, and the button now follows the new dates.
