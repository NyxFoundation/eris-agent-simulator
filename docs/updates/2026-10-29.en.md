# 2026-10-29 How the reference price moves, and a fix to the reference runtime

## The reference price moves differently

Rules §3.2 has been amended to change how the reference price and its gaps move. The amended version was published on Oct 29 and takes effect on Nov 13. The live competition (Dec 1 – 7) runs under it. The practice environment has been switching over ahead of it, in steps, since Oct 28.

Until now, the moves already observed told you something about the direction of the moves to come. This change removes that. Scoring, prizes and the schedule do not change. The authoritative text is §3.2 and §2.7 at [ascon.dev/rules](https://ascon.dev/rules).

## What changes in the live competition (rules §3.2)

- The reference price no longer tends back to its level at the start of the epoch. The size of a block's move does not change
- A price gap does not recover; it stays to the end of the epoch. Until now 40 – 100% of a gap came back. The direction of a gap:
  - Lending incident and CDP incident: down
  - Spike: up, once
  - Crash: each gap goes up or down with even odds
- In CEX drift the reference price no longer keeps moving one way; its moves get larger for a while, with no direction
- The borrowers that get liquidated (the Aave and Liquity victims) are set up before the start in every regime. Their presence is not a clue to the regime

The numbers for each regime are in the [starter guide](../competition-start.en.md)'s regime table.

## What changes in the practice environment

The practice environment switches over without stopping. The chain, balances, positions and the practice standings carry on. There is no need to fetch `manifest.json` again.

- **Since Oct 28**: the reference price no longer tends back to its starting level. The practice environment is one chain that runs for a month, so a block's move is about an eleventh of the live competition's, which makes a day's range about the same as one live epoch's (rules §2.7 states that the two can differ this way)
- **From around Nov 2**: gaps stop recovering and go up or down with even odds, at 2 – 4%. The CEX drift episode becomes the larger-moves shape, as in the live competition. The switch happens at a block number, so the date moves with how the chain advances

## A fix to the reference runtime

`obs.competition.maxCompetitorPriorityFeeWei` (the highest priority fee somebody else paid in the latest block) included the environment's own calls that have nothing to do with the auction, such as the GMX keeper. The reference `adaptive-arb` / `max-profit-arb` bid above that value and paid about twice what they needed to. The runtime now leaves those calls out; `maxBlockPriorityFeeWei` likewise.

- Rebuild your submission ZIP to pick it up (`npm run bundle:agent <id>`)
- If you run an agent on your own machine against the practice environment, pull the latest main and restart it

## Please

Public-set results from before and after this change cannot be compared: the same seed now moves differently. Pull the latest main and check again.

We recommend revisiting strategies built on any of these assumptions:

- the price returns to its level at the start of the epoch
- the price comes back after a sharp move
- the presence of victims tells the regimes apart
- in CEX drift the price keeps moving one way
